About this Framework

The categorisation assessment is not intended to rate hospitals, establish clinical rankings, or serve as a public quality assessment mechanism.

The objective of this exercise is to provide a common reference structure that can be used by healthcare providers and insurers for:

  • 1Improve trust between Healthcare Providers & Insurers
  • 2Strengthen the contractual engagement for serving policyholders
  • 3Reduce friction in the contract management between providers and payers
  • 4Create a foundation of trust for a framework approach to contract agreements and management
